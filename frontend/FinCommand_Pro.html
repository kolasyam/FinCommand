Management Commentary
Revenue grew 18.4% to ₹284.6 Cr driven by IT Services (+19.6%) and Managed Services (+19.3%). 18 new customer additions. Strong ARR growth in Healthcare (+28%) and EdTech (+32%) verticals.
EBITDA margin expanded to 12.4% (from 10.9%) through operating leverage. Employee cost as % of revenue held flat at 31.1% despite 122 new hires. Gross margin improved 130 bps to 52.1%.
Free Cash Flow ₹41.4 Cr. OCF/PAT = 1.86× confirms high earnings quality. Treasury ₹105 Cr. All bank covenants met with significant headroom. Net Debt position: Net Cash (debt-free).
Customer concentration risk: Top 10 customers = 64.8% of revenue. GovProject A margin 3.8% (below cost of capital). Recommend repricing or structured exit at renewal.
HDFC FD ₹15 Cr matured 31 Mar 2025 — renewal / redeployment decision pending. MSME payables overdue >45 days ₹80L — MSMED Act exposure. Requires immediate settlement.
FY26 Guidance: Revenue ₹340 Cr (+19.5%), EBITDA margin 14.5%, headcount +220, capex ₹22 Cr. Scenario modelling shows PAT range ₹22–38 Cr under bear/bull assumptions.
